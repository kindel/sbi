SBI

You owe someone feedback and the draft is still about the person. Rebuild it as Situation, Behavior, and Impact — words about what happened, not who they are.

1 Start with what you'd actually say

Vent here. Nothing you type leaves this page. The highlights show where the draft is about the person instead of what happened.

    2 Situation

    When and where. Specific enough to jog memory without debate — a date, a meeting, a document.

      3 Behavior

      What you saw or heard. Actions, not intent. A camera could have recorded it.

        4 Impact

        The effect on you, others, or the work. Say it from your side: “I noticed…”, “It cost us…”.

          5 Say this

          Your feedback appears here as you fill in the three parts.
          Before you deliver it
          • Soon and in private. Feedback ages badly.
          • Say the three parts, ask the intent question, then stop talking.
          • This works for praise too — positive SBI notes become the evidence in a promotion case.
